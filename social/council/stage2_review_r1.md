In #7 the trinity is mapped as spirit=direction, soul=being, body=strength, but the text's order ("Spēks, tava virzība un esība") gives spirit=strength, soul=direction, body=being.

**Arc:** Coherent, but #8 and #9 both cover the "field" idea.

## Response B
**Fidelity:** The best coverage of the source. It keeps "bezdarbība ietekmē kolektīvo lauku" (#9), "maldi / nesalūzt" (#12), "stiepekņi" (#13) and the full keys passage. #4 ("You stacked the wood. You can restack it.") is the best single line in any of the three answers.
**Rules:** No temperatures or hours, and "no clock on the wall" is a smart way to handle ritual length. Three soft risks: "Pirts works all three" (#8) edges toward a claim, "No music, no talking in the steam" (#11) states a fact about the ritual that may not be true, and "Not luck. Intention." (#6) drifts toward manifesting/motivational talk.
**Hooks / LV:** These are the strongest hooks: true 6-liners with admissions ("Es arī.") and a payoff that is always delivered. Weak spots: "Septītā diena 👇" is an empty ending, and "Trīs pazīmes, ka nav" is slightly clipped.
**Production / VO:** Many points are too long for the screen ("Strong enough not to break — not strong enough to fight everything"). Several VOs run 75–80 words inside 25–29 s (#1, #9), which is too tight. Pirts appears in almost every reel, and some tie-ins are forced (#2 "small wooden door", #6 clock).
**Arc:** The most deliberate arc (AM idea / PM consequence), but #5 and #6 split a single sentence about intention and overlap.

## Response C
**Fidelity:** Good. "Invisible threads" captures "stiepekņi", and #3 keeps the line "doing nothing still affects everything". Moving the keys to Day 2 gives #14 room, but it breaks the text's own order of ending.
**Rules:** The cleanest of the three. Pirts appears in about 5 reels, and each one feels natural ("you hear the stones, the water, your breath").
**Hooks / LV:** The Latvian is the most natural ("Palaid vaļā", "Spēcīgie nelūzt. Viņi liecas kā koks"). Problems: in #2, "skriet... sēdēt" mixes two metaphors. #8 and #14 add "prāts", which is not in the text. The #14 hook is a meta recap that cannot stand alone in a feed. #6 puts "vienkārši nenotiek" in quotation marks, which reads like a calque.
**Production / VO:** Points are short and screen-ready, and the VOs (50–60 words) fit their lengths. Some images are hard to source: "hand pulling back from hot sand in an hourglass" and "an ear-shaped knot in birch bark".
**Arc:** Clear and distinct. The "Come and feel it" ending is slightly salesy.

## 3 weakest
1. **A#9**: a 4-line hook (below spec), the awkward "Pamēģini simts", and a thin VO.
2. **C#14**: the meta hook does not work standalone, it adds "prāts", and the CTA reads like an ad.
3. **B#6**: it duplicates #5, "Not luck. Intention." sounds like manifesting, and the VO is too long for 28 s.

## Disagreements
- **Trinity mapping:** A uses direction/being/strength, while B and C use strength/direction/being. **B and C are right**, because they follow the order of the text.
- **Circumstances:** A says they are "not yours", while B and C say "we build them". **B and C are right.** A inverts his meaning.
- **Keys placement:** A and B put the keys on Day 7 next to the oneness line, while C puts them on Day 2. **A and B are slightly better**, because they keep his ending sequence. B's split across 5 points works better than A's crammed version.
- **Pirts density:** B puts pirts in nearly every reel, while A and C use it in about half. **A and C are right**, as the brief says not to force it.
- **Pilot:** A and B choose letting go + tree, while C chooses keys + ears. **Letting go (B#3) + ears (C#9)** is the strongest pair, testing one idea reel and one practical reel. The tree reel is safe but low-curiosity.
- **VO density:** B uses about 75–80 words and A/C about 45–60. **A and C are right** for 22–28 s reels.
