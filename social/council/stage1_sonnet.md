## 1. Concept and 7-day arc

Concept: Mārcis speaks as a man who sits in silence with steam and fire, not as a coach. Each reel is one plain, felt idea from his text, pointed at one image at a time. Pirts shows up as a place where the idea is lived, never as the sales pitch.
Arc: from "right now" to "I am not only my body", moving from presence, to choice, to body and spirit, to others, to filtering, to letting go.

- Day 1, Presence. AM: how you feel right now. PM: the future forms in this moment.
- Day 2, Choice. AM: old beliefs and the wrong train. PM: let go, but still act.
- Day 3, Intention and body. AM: put intention into any action. PM: the tree that bends in the wind.
- Day 4, Spirit and oneness. AM: spirit, soul, body. PM: "all is one" is not doing nothing.
- Day 5, Hands and field. AM: joined hands change the field. PM: whoever lets go, goes elsewhere.
- Day 6, Filter. AM: clean ears, nothing in them. PM: pixels, projections, one real.
- Day 7, Keys and oneness. AM: do not be drained, ask "does this resonate". PM: old keys, and "I am not my body".

## 3. Pilot pair

Best pilots: #6 (tree in the wind) and #4 (letting go is not doing nothing).
- #6 is the most visual. The tree is a concrete image, the idea is in his own words, and it carries no abstract claims. It tests the pointing format with minimal risk.
- #4 is the most "him": a clear contrast hook, a real insight (letting go versus acting), and strong comment potential. It shows whether his voice holds attention when the idea is a little deeper.
Together they test one concrete and one conceptual reel.

## 4. Risks and mitigations

- Cultish or "we are all one" tone (#8, #9, #12, #14). Mitigation: every such reel starts from an ordinary question or image, uses "I" language ("I choose", "I ask myself"), and never tells the viewer to join or follow anything. The final reel's "I am everything" is the only place it is stated flat, after six days of grounding.
- Preachy imperatives (#6, #11, #13). Mitigation: hooks are questions or contrasts, and the VO says "I do" more than "you must".
- Vague abstractions: "field", "projections", "truth". Mitigation: each is paired with a physical picture (ripples, mirrors, a cracked pane) and a small concrete action (silence, one question).
- Pirts forced in. Mitigation: only about half the reels mention it, and only as a place (bench, stove, steam), never as a method or a promise. No medical claims, no alcohol, no temperatures, no fixed durations.
- Latvian that sounds translated. Mitigation: hooks use short spoken phrases. A native editor should still check them, especially #12 ("spogulis/atspulgi") and #3.

## 5. Confidence

Medium. The arc and angles are solid, but the final Latvian and the VO tone depend on how Mārcis actually speaks.
The one thing that would most change the plan: hearing a sample of his real speech rhythm. If he is sparse and blunt, I would cut the VO to 30-40 words and use fewer points. If he is warm and flowing, I would add one extra point to several reels.
