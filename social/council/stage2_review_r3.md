## Disagreements
- Trinity mapping: A vs B/C. B/C are right; the text lists spirit/soul/body then strength/direction/being in parallel.
- Keys placement: C (Day 2) vs A/B (Day 7). A/B are right; "tikko licies, ka jauns, bet tagad ir vecs... esam tam gatavi" is a closing statement and the arc needs it as the landing.
- Hands/"who lets go": C merges, A/B split. A/B are right; distinctness rule, and the "lets go" line needs its own de-threatening treatment (B#10's "don't grab, don't punish" is the best version).
- Pilot pair: A #6+#4, B #3+#7, C #4+#9. B's reasoning (concrete pirts payoff, test mechanics first) is soundest; C's keys reel is the better single object, so B#3 + C#4 is the ideal pair.
- Intention line: C reads it literally (add action to intention), A/B invert it. Practically a wash; the VO should say both halves.
