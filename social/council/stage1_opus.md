# Saimnieku Pirtis: 14-reel "pointing" series

## 1. Concept + 7-day arc
**Concept:** "Notes from the pirts bench." Mārcis says out loud what he has learned from years of steam, silence and people. Each reel takes one line from his text and turns it into one picture you can carry home.
**Arc:** it moves from inside to outside. It starts with *now*, then goes through change, intention, body, senses and the field, and ends with *oneness*. Day 7 closes where the text closes.

| 2 | Change | #3 Letting go is not doing nothing | #4 Rusty keys |

## 2. The 14 reels

#1 | Day 1 AM | "Jūsu sajūta un esība šobrīd… Nākotne top momentā."
HOOK_LV: Tava nākotne / netop rīt. / Tā top tagad, / šajā elpā. / Paskaties, kā 👇
HOOK_EN: Your future / isn't made tomorrow. / It's made now, / in this breath. / Here's how 👇
POINTS: Notice how you feel right now / Every moment you start again / The future is built in this second
POINT_IMAGES: steam rising off the bench in soft light / a single birch leaf on wet wood / a doorway opening onto a morning field
VO_EN: "Stop for a second. How do you actually feel right now? Not yesterday, not tonight. Now. Every moment we come back to zero, we get another start. That's the good news. The future isn't waiting somewhere ahead. It's made right here, in this moment, out of how you are. So start with this one."
LENGTH: 22 s

#2 | Day 1 PM | "Ja nemainīs savu ticības sistēmu, reakcijas. Liela iespēja būt neīstajā vilcienā."

HOOK_EN: You can run / your whole life / and still sit / on the wrong train. / Here's why 👇
POINTS: Same beliefs = same destination / Watch your automatic reactions / Change the reaction, change the track
POINT_IMAGES: an old train on a misty track / a hand pulling back from hot sand in an hourglass (reaction) / a rail switch at a fork in the tracks
VO_EN: "If you never question what you believe, and you keep reacting the same way, there's a big chance you're on the wrong train. And it's comfortable, so you don't notice. Look at your reactions. That's where the old beliefs live. Change one reaction, and the rails change under you. Small switch, completely different place."
LENGTH: 25 s

#3 | Day 2 AM | "Viss ir viens — tā nav bezdarbība… ir jāpalaižas, bet nenozīmē, ka nevajag darīt."

HOOK_EN: "Let go" / doesn't mean / "do nothing." / That's the biggest / mistake 👇

#4 | Day 2 PM | "No rokām krīt daudz atslēgas… nelabojam — nomainām…"

Don't sit there repairing them. Replace them. It changes completely. And honestly? You already know which keys I mean. We're ready for this."

#5 | Day 3 AM | "Verot un klausoties sevi un citos, pielikt nodomam klāt jebkuru darbību…"

## 3. Best pilot pair
**#4 Rusty keys** and **#9 Clean ears.**
- #4 is the most visual and the most "him". The keys are a concrete object he can point at, the line "don't repair, replace" is quotable, and it shows the depth of the series without sounding esoteric.
- #9 is the most practical. It gives the viewer a one-minute challenge, connects naturally to the pirts experience (stones, water, breath), and makes a good save/share. It also tests whether the audience takes simple, practical ideas better than abstract ones.
- Together they test two kinds of hook, metaphor and question, before the full 14 are produced.

## 4. Risks and mitigations
- **Sounding cultish** ("collective field", "holy trinity", "oneness"). I gave every abstraction a concrete image (firewood, a tuning fork, a ring of hands) and an everyday example (family, village). #13 adds "no blame, it's a choice" so letting go of hands doesn't read as a threat. "Holy trinity" is framed as "I see it as", his personal view, not doctrine.
- **Preachy.** The VOs are first-person and observational ("I see", "you feel it too") and avoid commands where possible. #2 and #12 end on an open choice, not a verdict.
- **Vague.** Every reel has one takeaway action or question: notice how you feel, change one reaction, attach one action, a minute of silence, two questions.
- **Health and other rule risks.** "Strengthen your body" is about standing, not health outcomes. No heat numbers, no durations, no alcohol, no claims about detox or healing. Pirts appears only where it fits naturally (#4, #8, #9, #11, #14).
- **Anti-screen hypocrisy** (#9 and #11 run on a phone). Handle it with a light touch: in #11, Mārcis can point at the phone *while on* the phone, with a small smile.
- **Latvian.** The hooks are written natively, not translated. "Palaid vaļā" and "ne tajā vilcienā" are everyday phrases. A native editor should still do a final read for rhythm.

## 5. Confidence
**Medium.** The structure and the source mapping are solid. Tone and hook strength depend on how Mārcis delivers on camera. **What would most change the plan:** seeing his pilot delivery. If his presence is strongly calm and spiritual, the more abstract reels (#8, #14) can go deeper. If he comes across better when practical, I would swap the order of Days 4–5, put "senses" earlier, and make the hooks more concrete and challenge-based.
