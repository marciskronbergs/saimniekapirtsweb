Hooks are the most curiosity-driven. Weak spots: pirts is sometimes forced and invented ("No steam is thrown without intention", "Steam for the body, silence for the soul, fire and breath for the spirit", "small wooden door in the forest"). #7 "cold lake after the steam" and "lake plunge" brush the temperature-contrast ban. #6 overclaims ("Not luck. LV hooks natural. Images are filmable with real pirts assets (whisk binding, ladle, door). Arc and distinctness strong.

## Response C
Fidelity: shortest, cleanest hooks ("Tava nākotne netop rīt", "Ja atslēga vairs neder, tu to nomaini"), VOs 45-55 words, points short enough for screen. Only response to read "pielikt nodomam klāt darbību" correctly (#5). Weak spots: #14 hook is meta ("Septiņas dienas mēs runājām par ķermeni, prātu, lauku", and "prāts" is not in his text) and ends on a sales CTA "Come feel it yourself", which reads cultish/salesy. #1 ends on a limp "Paskaties, kā". #8 "not just a mind" bends his trinity. Keys moved to Day 2 and hands to Day 7, so the arc is looser than the text's own order. #2 image "hot sand" is a stray heat word. #12 LV "ir domāta" agreement is slightly off. Pirts tie-ins mostly natural (#9 stones/water/breath), #11 "steam is so honest" a bit poetic. Arc table is neat.

## Disagreements
1. Pirts density: B ties nearly every reel, A and C about half. A/C are right on restraint; B's grounding helps producibility, but several of its ties are invented doctrine.
2. Intention/action direction: C is right (action added to the intention); A and B inverted it.
3. Teaser hooks ("Three signs", "Two questions"): B uses them, A/C don't. B is right when delivered (#13, #12-type) and wrong when mismatched (#2).
4. Text order: A follows it, C reshuffles. A is right; keys and "I am everything" belong at the end.
5. Closing: B's reflective sign-off beats C's invitation CTA and A's flat ending.

## Five best reels
1. B#4: "Tādi nu ir apstākļi" is the most natural, relatable LV hook, and "ownership is light" is his thought.
2. C#4: keys hook is one concrete object, quotable "replace, don't repair".
3. B#11: earbuds hook is behavioural, filmable, and it ties to pirts silence without forcing.
4. A#4: letting go is not doing nothing, true to source with a clean contrast.
5. C#2: "skriet visu dzīvi, ne tajā vilcienā" is a tight hook with a plain, voiced VO.

## Three weakest reels
1. C#14: meta hook with no curiosity, "prātu" off-source, ends on a sales CTA.
2. B#6: hook promises "one difference" and the VO answers "Intention, not luck", an overclaim that borders on clickbait.
3. A#9: 4-line hook, "try a hundred" is thin, VO is generic.
