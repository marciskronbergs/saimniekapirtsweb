# Saimnieku Pirtis — 14 "pointing" reels, 7-day arc

## 1. Series concept + arc

**Concept:** "Viens lauks / One Field" — seven days that walk from the smallest unit (how you feel this second) outward to the shared field, and end on Mārcis's own closing line, "I am not my body. All is one." Every abstract sentence of his is pinned to one physical thing he can point at in or around the pirts — stone, water, whisk, door, key — so the spirit talk stays grounded, never floats.

**Arc (AM = inner/idea, PM = consequence/practice):**
- Day 1 — NOW. AM: your feeling right now makes the future. PM: the wrong train.
- Day 2 — CHANGE. AM: letting go ≠ doing nothing. PM: who built "the circumstances".
- Day 3 — INTENTION. AM: watch, listen, then add intention. PM: why things move fast for some.
- Day 4 — BODY. AM: the tree that bends. PM: spirit–soul–body trinity.
- Day 5 — HANDS / FIELD. AM: joined hands change the field. PM: when someone lets go.
- Day 6 — EARS / FILTER. AM: take the earbuds out. PM: you will see what's false — don't break.
- Day 7 — TRUTH. AM: mirrors, projections, two questions. PM: rusted keys; I am all.

HOOK_EN: How do you feel / right now? / Not yesterday. Not tomorrow. / This very moment. / Because the future / is made now 👇
POINTS: p1 Your state right now is the raw material / p2 Every moment, you return to it / p3 The future isn't out there — it's being made this second / p4 In pirts we start here: how are you, now?
POINT_IMAGES: p1 hands resting on knees on a pirts bench / p2 single drop hitting a wet stone / p3 steam rising in a dark pirts / p4 Mārcis at the pirts door greeting a guest
VO_EN: Stop for one second. How do you feel right now? Not yesterday, not how you hope to feel tomorrow. Now. Every moment we come back to this. The future isn't waiting somewhere ahead — it's being made in this exact second, out of this exact feeling. In the pirts that's the first thing I ask. Not what you want. How are you, right now?

## 3. Pilot pair

**#3 (letting go ≠ doing nothing) and #7 (the tree that bends).** They are the two most concrete reels — each has a physical pirts object as the payoff (whisk-binding, birch branch), so the "pointing" format is tested with real assets, not paintings. One is mind, one is body, so the pilot checks both halves of the arc. Neither uses a single esoteric word (no "field", no "projection"), which makes them the fairest test of whether the hook/pointing mechanics work before the harder Day 5–7 material. Reserve #11 (earbuds) as the third if one pilot underperforms — it has the most universal, behavioural hook.

## 4. Risks and mitigations

- **Cultish ("the field", "we are ready", "the one who leaves goes elsewhere").** Every "field" line is immediately scaled down to the smallest observable version (two hands, one breath, a ripple). #10 explicitly says don't grab, don't punish — leaving is normal. "We are ready" in #14 is kept as his sentence, in the last five seconds, not repeated as a slogan. No in-group words (awakening, frequency, vibration) anywhere.
- **Preachy.** Hooks are questions or admissions ("Me too", "I've said it too", "the biggest mistake I see"). VO uses "I" for the pirts lines ("my place to check what's true is…") and offers, never prescribes: "find yours", "which one needs you".
- **Vague.** Rule applied: no abstract sentence without a thing to point at. "All is one" is only allowed as the closing line after six days of concrete images, not as a Day-1 premise.
- **Translated Latvian.** Hooks were written in LV first as speech, short clauses, no calques ("Palaist vaļā nenozīmē nedarīt neko", "Tādi nu ir apstākļi"). His own word "rezonē" stays in the VO (it's his), but the LV hook uses "der tev / palīdz atšķirt".
- **Compliance.** No temperatures, no durations ("no clock on the wall — as long as it needs"), no health or healing claims (the lake and whisks are described as ritual, not treatment), no alcohol.
- **Clickbait.** "Three signs", "two questions", "one difference" are always delivered in the points.

## 5. Confidence

**Medium-high** on structure and voice; **medium** on the English VO over Latvian hooks. The one thing that would most change the plan: **who actually watches** — if the account's audience is mainly Latvian, the English VO becomes the weak link and I'd flip it (LV voice, EN captions) and shorten every reel by one point. If the pilots show the hook-to-3-second retention is driven by question hooks over statement hooks, I'd rewrite #7, #9, #12, #14 as questions.
